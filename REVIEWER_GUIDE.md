# CareHire Docs — Prototype Review Guide

Thank you for reviewing CareHire Docs.

Please try to complete the prototype as if you were a family member who needs to hire private help at home.

## Suggested test

1. Choose the type of worker you need.
2. Select your province.
3. Choose several care needs.
4. Generate the estimated weekly hours.
5. Enter a schedule and pay rate.
6. Review the generated job description.
7. Review the interview questions.
8. Review the draft work agreement.
9. Try voice input and read-aloud if your browser supports them.

## Feedback we want

- Was anything confusing?
- Did you know what to do next on each screen?
- Did the hours estimate feel useful?
- Would you use the generated job description?
- Were the interview questions useful?
- Did the work agreement feel understandable?
- Did anything feel too legal, technical, or complicated?
- What would stop you from using this tool?
- What is the one thing you would change first?

This prototype is for product testing only and does not provide legal advice.
